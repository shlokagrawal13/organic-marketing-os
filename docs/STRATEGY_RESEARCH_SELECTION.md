# Strategy research selection

RESEARCH-01D adds optional `researchSelection` to strategy enqueue: up to six categories, one market label (100 characters) and one language label (80 characters). Empty choices mean all values. Categories are combined with OR; market and language are combined with AND. Labels match whole values ignoring case, with surrounding request whitespace removed. There is no substring matching, translation or locale inference. Literal %, underscore and backslash remain literal PostgreSQL filter values.

The tenant query applies these filters before the deterministic latest-100 candidate limit. Capture then retains at most 10 eligible reviewed sources within the existing 24,000-byte snapshot budget. Unreviewed, changed, unauthorized, expired and foreign-tenant evidence remains excluded. The bounded window can still omit older eligible matching sources; it is not an exhaustive library search or a relevance ranking.

Canonical choices are stored in job input when supplied and in every new strategy research snapshot, including an empty result. The worker, provider request and trace carry the captured choices. Editing the form or library does not recapture a queued job. Replaying an equivalent normalized request returns the original job; changing choices with the same request key returns conflict. Content and scene requests reject this strategy-only field. Clients cannot supply a research snapshot or arbitrary source IDs.

The strategy form exposes category, market and language filters and a clear action. The source viewer shows captured filters independently of the current form. Missing matching evidence stays visible and generated recommendations must declare research gaps or assumptions. Older snapshots without choices remain readable and are explicitly labeled as having no recorded filters; they do not acquire today's choices.

Existing source revision/fingerprint/expiry checks, provider-boundary checks and approval-time source checks remain in place. Snapshot readers also reject evidence inconsistent with captured filters. Selection and source context persist through tenant export and populated archive restore without changing any migration.

Selection restricts input. It does not certify source truth, rights, quality, relevance, semantic entailment or live model quality. Live source adapters and full master acceptance remain open.
