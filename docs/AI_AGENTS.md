# AI orchestration

Implemented tasks: marketing strategy, content/script generation, and an individual scene rewrite. New jobs preserve the organization's Brand Brain/Creative DNA, up to ten recent approved content examples and their revision-time values when queued. The worker uses that frozen snapshot and delegates the one generative step through the centralized ModelRouter. Structured outputs are validated before being accepted.

Version 0.8 implements a durable task-specific graph rather than prompt-only labels. All 18 master roles have explicit responsibilities and are mapped across strategy, content and scene plans. Context roles execute as bounded deterministic steps, the selected strategy/script/editor role owns the validated provider generation, compliance and orchestrator critique run afterward, and a human gate must approve the result before the UI can turn it into a draft or apply a scene rewrite. `AIAgentRun` and `AIAgentStep` retain frozen context, dependencies, state transitions, outputs and linked provider usage; tenant-scoped trace export exposes the complete record.

This local orchestration does not fabricate capabilities. Research and community roles explicitly record that external evidence was not supplied; publishing never posts automatically; analytics/growth roles produce context and measurement requirements rather than invented performance. Live research sources, platform publishing, external analytics, generated media and live-model quality remain their separate task-board gates.

The model receives structured business data and bounded approved examples, not a historical conversation dump. It is instructed to treat brand/input fields as data, avoid inventing external evidence or current trends, record assumptions and preserve scene IDs. Automated checks block obvious guaranteed-results language, but they are not factual or policy verification. OWNER/ADMIN/EDITOR review is durable and auditable; blocked compliance results cannot be approved.

Legacy queued jobs created before migration 6 have no snapshot and use the current brand. Worker heartbeats, ownership tokens, abort/deadline handling and no-replay failure semantics are documented in MODEL_ROUTER.md. Fixture tests establish contracts and failure behavior, not live model quality.
